1. Water
	H-bond: 2.8A
	Titration curve of conjugate acid-base pairs: Buffering regions (pKa±1)
		Henderson-Hasselbach equation: pH=pKa+lg([A-]/[HA])
		Biological buffer system: in vivo : Phosphate (cytoplasm) / Bicarbonate (blood plasma); in vitro : Tris, HEPES, PIPES (sulfonic acids of cyclic amines)

2. Amino acids, Peptides, Proteins
	3.1 Amino acids
		L-α-aa (except Gly), 110 Da in average
		Classes
			Nonpolar, aliphatic
				Glycine Gly G (-H), Alanine Ala A (-Me), Proline Pro P (-CH2-CH2-+NH2-), Valine Val V (-CH(Me)2), Leucine Leu L (-CH2-CH(Me)2), Isoleucine Ile I (-CHMe-CH2CH3), Methionine Met M (-CH2-CH2-S-CH3)
			Aromatic: UV absorption at 270-280 nm
				Phenylalanine Phe F (-Ph), Tyrosine Tyr Y (-Ph-OH), Tryptophan Trp W (-CH2-indole ring)
			Polar, uncharged
				Serine Ser S, Threonine Thr T, Cystine Cys C, Asparagine Asn N, Glutamine Gln Q
			Polar, positive
				Arginine Arg R, Lysine Lys K, Histidine His H
			Polar, negative
				Aspartate Asp D, Glutamate Glu E
