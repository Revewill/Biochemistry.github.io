Bars: estimates from mean unknown absorbance.
Error bars: 95% calibration intervals, NOT SD or SEM.
Intervals invert prediction limits for the mean of the unknown readings.
Assumptions: valid linear calibration; independent normal equal-variance response errors.
Unknown response variance is estimated from calibration residuals.
Standard concentrations and dilution factors are treated as exact.
Pipetting, dilution uncertainty, shared preparation errors and model misspecification are not included.
Identical A280 readings do not imply zero calibration uncertainty.
BCA intervals are conditional on the linear model despite residual curvature.
Both BCA estimates share a calibration; they are not independent biological groups.
Undiluted BCA extrapolations are excluded; no pooled BCA estimate is reported.
No significance test is performed; interval overlap does not establish equivalence.
